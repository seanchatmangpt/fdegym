# fdegym v2030.1.1 — PRD / ARD

**Repository:** `seanchatmangpt/fdegym`  
**Ecosystem role:** capstone / Fortune 5 FDE qualification  
**Depends on:** `affidavit`, `autofde`, `autofde-lab`, `gymact`, `mfact`, `mfw`, `wasm4pm`  
**Release:** Chatman Ecosystem v2030.1.1  
**Standing conferred:** `UNKNOWN`.

## Product requirement

`fdegym` SHALL be the capstone qualification environment for proving that the Chatman Ecosystem can perform Forward Deployed Engineering at Fortune 5 complexity without collapsing simulation, formal proof, orchestration, production actuation, and evidence into one unverifiable claim.

By v2030.1.1, FDEGym must test complete enterprise problems spanning cloud, applications, process, marketplace commerce, identity, security, data, cost, policy, failure, and organizational constraints.

## Required capabilities

1. Compose executable worlds/actions from `gymact` with real or faithful enterprise-scale scenarios.
2. Exercise graduated AutoFDE policies and compare them against exploration baselines from `autofde-lab`.
3. Require process execution evidence from `wasm4pm` when scenarios depend on process behavior.
4. Require MFW orchestration/authority boundaries for consequential multi-stage scenarios.
5. Consume `mfact` certificates only for claims they actually prove.
6. Use `affidavit` receipts to compose exact evidence DAGs across all scenario boundaries.
7. Model Fortune 5 constraints: multi-cloud/region, sovereign boundaries, IAM, network segmentation, regulated data, vendor contracts, enterprise marketplaces, commitments, SLOs, budgets, incident conditions, and rollback/recovery.
8. Include adversarial, chaos, partial-observation, contradictory-input, and provider-failure episodes.
9. Score evidence completeness and lawful refusal in addition to task completion, cost, latency, and reliability.

## Architecture requirements

```text
Fortune 5 scenario capsule
      -> admitted enterprise world
      -> role/information/authority partition
      -> candidate policy/architecture set
      -> gym + live-boundary execution lanes
      -> formal/process/orchestration evidence
      -> postcondition + economic evaluation
      -> affidavit receipt DAG
      -> scoped standing
```

The capstone SHALL distinguish environment fidelity from product success. A gym result cannot by itself prove a live cloud/marketplace boundary. A live operation cannot by itself prove global architecture correctness.

## Required benchmark families

v2030.1.1 SHALL include representative suites for:

- greenfield global deployment;
- brownfield discovery/reconstitution;
- cloud migration and portability;
- marketplace procurement/entitlement/fulfillment;
- security vulnerability remediation;
- cost/FinOps optimization;
- incident diagnosis/recovery;
- application/process modernization;
- data-residency and regulatory constraint satisfaction;
- multi-provider outage and degraded-mode operation.

## Acceptance for v2030.1.1

A crown scenario is complete only when the exact scenario subject demonstrates:

- admitted observations and world identity;
- policy/planner identity;
- explicit authority partition;
- execution at every boundary named by the claim;
- typed refusals for illegal alternatives;
- hard-constraint satisfaction;
- postcondition verification;
- cost/performance metrics;
- receipt DAG closure;
- replay or explicit bounded non-replayability;
- no evidence class promoted beyond what its owning verifier established.

At least one capstone SHALL cross materially different cloud/marketplace/provider families without changing canonical product semantics.

## Falsifiers

The architecture is falsified if benchmark scores can be maximized by bypassing authority or evidence; if simulated provider behavior is reported as live; if scenario fixtures encode the answer; if one component's ALIVE status is inherited without exact subject correspondence; or if failure/refusal correctness is penalized as generic task failure.

## Ecosystem contract

FDEGym is the **integration court**, not the owner of every underlying claim. It composes evidence from component authorities and proves only the exact end-to-end scenario boundaries it executes.
